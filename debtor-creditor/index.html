Debtor / Creditor Consolidation

Lists every debtor (or creditor) as at a date across all your group companies — many companies in one Tally, or several Tally instances open separately — matches the same party across their books (by PAN, GSTIN or name), and lays the balances side by side: particulars · balance per company · total. Debtors show credit balances as negatives; creditors show debit balances as negatives. Everything flows browser ↔ connector ↔ Tally on this computer.

Looking for the connector…
The KNAP connector runs on the computer where Tally Prime is installed.

1 · Which Tally endpoints to read

Each running Tally exposes one port (default 9000). Add a line for every Tally you have open separately (9001, 9002…). One Tally holding several companies needs just its one line.

2 · What to extract

Balances are read one company at a time — never all at once — from books-start up to the date you set, so the closing figure is exact.
Every party is matched automatically, in this order — 1. GSTIN  →  2. PAN  →  3. exact name  →  4. smart name (pvt = private, ltd = limited, & = and…)  →  5. similar names (surfaced for you to confirm). Parties belonging to your own group companies are flagged INTER-CO.

3 · Match & review

Confident matches (same PAN / GSTIN / name) are merged automatically. Confirm the look-alikes below, then merge or split any row in the sheet by hand. Your decisions are remembered for next time.

4 · Consolidated sheet
