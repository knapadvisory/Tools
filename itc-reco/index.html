GSTR-2B vs Books ITC Reconciliation

Reads your input-GST ledgers straight from Tally (invoice-wise), matches them to your downloaded GSTR-2B — and GSTR-2A too, if you add it — and shows what’s matched, mismatched, only in the return, or only in your books. Supplier credit notes are checked against the reversals in your books. Everything runs on this computer — books flow browser ↔ connector ↔ Tally; only the report you export is saved.

1 · Connect to Tally

Keep each company on the Gateway of Tally (not inside a report). Add a line per Tally port (9000, 9001…), then scan. Tick all the companies you want to reconcile — books are pulled from each and matched within its own GSTIN. If one company holds several GST registrations under one PAN (a shared Input-GST ledger with entries for all of them), just tick that one company — each voucher's own registration is read from Tally and split out automatically.

1b · Or — a remote / hosted Tally: start from its export

Tally shown through a remote-app window has no port this tool can reach. Inside that window, from the Gateway press Alt+E → Transactions (not Current, and not the Registers — those export only the columns on screen). Type of Voucher entries: All Vouchers — TallyPrime has no voucher-type filter here, so the file holds sales and receipts too; that is fine, only the vouchers touching the ledgers you tag are read. Include dependent masters: Yes — that puts each ledger’s group and GSTIN in the file, so a bill booked as a Journal (no party field) still gets its supplier and GSTIN. Format XML (Data Interchange), Folder Path on your own drive (not the server’s Program Files), and the Period — one month at a time if a year is too large a file; drop all twelve here together. The file is the company: every ledger in it is listed in step 2 for you to tag, and nothing above needs to be connected.
Drop the Tally export .xml file(s) here, or click to choose.

2 · Pick the input-GST ledgers

Tag each input ledger you claim ITC through as IGST / CGST / SGST — and use the RCM options for reverse-charge ledgers. Likely input ledgers are pre-tagged, but check them: output/liability and rate ledgers are NOT auto-selected — only ones named “Input…”. Debits = ITC availed, credits = reversals (both netted). Then Confirm.

4 · Add your GSTR-2B (and GSTR-2A, if you have it)

Upload the GSTR-2B JSON(s) from the GST portal — you can add several months — or a filled CSV template. GSTR-2A JSON works too: drop both and each file is recognised on its own. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; parsed in your browser.
Reverse-charge invoices in 2B are matched against your RCM ledgers.
Drop your GSTR-2B / GSTR-2A .json file(s), the portal's .zip exactly as downloaded, or a template .csv here — or click to choose. Add as many months as you need.
Downloading twelve months by hand? There is a way to capture them from the portal in one go — it runs in your own logged-in tab and never sees a password.
Which tax heads are you reconciling?
2B carries every invoice, whichever head it fell under. If you pulled only some input-GST ledgers from the books, leave the rest unticked — otherwise their 2B invoices come back as “not in books”, which is not a finding, only a gap in what was fetched.
Tie out to the GST portal — optional
The portal’s “ITC auto-drafted in GSTR-2B” figure, per registration. Give it and the Excel gains a Tie-out sheet that reconciles this tool’s 2B figure to GSTN’s, line by line. Leave blank to skip — the sheet is still produced, showing what the total is made of.
