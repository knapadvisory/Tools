GSTR-2B vs Books ITC Reconciliation

Reads your input-GST ledgers straight from Tally (invoice-wise), matches them to your downloaded GSTR-2B, and shows what’s matched, mismatched, only in 2B, or only in your books. Everything runs on this computer — books flow browser ↔ connector ↔ Tally; only the report you export is saved.

1 · Connect to Tally

Keep each company on the Gateway of Tally (not inside a report). Add a line per Tally port (9000, 9001…), then scan. Tick all the companies you want to reconcile — books are pulled from each and matched within its own GSTIN. If one company holds several GST registrations under one PAN (a shared Input-GST ledger with entries for all of them), just tick that one company — each voucher's own registration is read from Tally and split out automatically.

2 · Pick the input-GST ledgers

Tag each input ledger you claim ITC through as IGST / CGST / SGST — and use the RCM options for reverse-charge ledgers. Likely input ledgers are pre-tagged, but check them: output/liability and rate ledgers are NOT auto-selected — only ones named “Input…”. Debits = ITC availed, credits = reversals (both netted). Then Confirm.
