Maintain the fixed-asset register below. Depreciation is charged on a pro-rata (day) basis from the date each asset is put to use, capped at the residual value, per Schedule II. The Excel export is your working paper — re-import it next year and use Roll forward to carry closing balances as opening.

Seed additions from Tally · optional

Run this on the PC where Tally is open. It pulls the year's debits to your Fixed-Asset ledgers as candidate additions (you then set life & method). Disposals and ledger balances are shown so you can reconcile the gross block.

Schedule II — Part C useful lives (reference)

Lives shown are the Schedule II defaults; a different life needs technical justification and disclosure. Continuous-process plant and specialised industry assets carry their own lives — pick “Custom” and enter the life.

Property, Plant & Equipment — note (Schedule III)

Block-wise movement for the note to accounts. Prior-year net block comes from the opening figures you entered.

Depreciation as per Income-tax Act (Section 32)

Block-of-assets WDV method. Additions and sale proceeds are pulled from the register above (assets put to use for < 180 days get half rate). Enter each block's opening WDV as per the last income-tax return.

Deferred tax (AS 22)

Timing differences between the books and income-tax, at the effective tax rate. The main one — depreciation — is computed from the WDV difference (book vs tax) above; add any others.
