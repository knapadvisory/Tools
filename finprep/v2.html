Import the books from Tally

Run this on the PC where Tally is open. The extract is stored as a sealed snapshot — it is never edited afterwards; corrections are made by journal.

…or upload a trial balance in Excel

For books that cannot be reached from this PC — a client’s Tally on a remote desktop, another accounting package, a trial balance that arrived as a spreadsheet. Download the template, fill one row per ledger (debit and credit in separate columns; opening and closing; movements optional), and upload it. The sheet is checked before anything is stored: it must balance, every ledger once, every head one the tool knows. A re-upload replaces the previous trial balance and shows what changed; the heads you approved and the adjustments you recorded are kept.

Partners and profit sharing

The year’s profit is divided by these ratios, to the paisa, and each partner’s capital account is prepared from their own ledgers — opening, capital introduced, drawings, interest, remuneration, share of profit, closing. Ledgers are matched by the partner’s name where they carry it; set the rest here. A manual share, if entered for every partner, replaces the ratio for this year and must add up to the profit.

Review the grouping

Every ledger, the Schedule III head it reached, and the line it will appear on in the note. Ledgers of the same nature share one note line — edit the caption to move a ledger onto a different line, or give it its own. Approved decisions are remembered for this engagement.

Adjustment entries

Journals passed on the trial balance after the books were read — provisions, reclassifications, corrections, closing entries. The books themselves are never edited: every entry is recorded here with its rationale, must balance to the paisa, and is applied only once approved. Entries are kept by ledger name, so they survive a re-import of the books; an entry that points at a ledger no longer in the books is flagged and blocks release until it is edited or withdrawn.

Sign-off

Who signs the statements. The auditor on the left of each face; the board or the owners on the right (the owners come from step 4). Kept with the engagement; printed on the Excel and the PDF.

Significant accounting policies

Printed as the first section of the notes. “Insert a starting text” gives a template for this constitution and framework with the choices to confirm marked [CONFIRM: …] — it is a draft to edit, never a statement of fact about the client.

Export

One workbook: Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, Notes, Trial Balance, Disclosures and Review. The faces are formula-linked to the notes and the notes to the trial balance, so every figure is traceable inside the file.
